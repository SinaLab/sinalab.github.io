UDHRBench

Benchmarking Human Rights in Large Language Models
مؤشر التزام النماذج اللغوية في الإعلان الدولي لحقوق الإنسان
Large-scale benchmark for operationalising the evaluation of large language model compliance with the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
Dataset: 1,124 questions and 4,496 responses from four LLMs (ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, and Fanar), manually annotated by experts - covering all 30 UDHR articles.
Method: LLM-as-a-Judge to evaluate new models automatically with 90% F1.
