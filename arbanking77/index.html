ArBanking77

ArBanking77 Intent Detection Corpus and Models
ArBanking77 consists of 31,404 (MSA, Palestinian, Saudi, Moroccan, and Tunisian dialects) that are manually Arabized and localized from the original English Banking77 dataset; which consists of 13,083 queries. Each query is classified into one of the 77 classes (intents) including card arrival, card linking, exchange rate, and automatic top-up. A neural model based on AraBERT was fine-tuned the ArBanking77 dataset (F1-score 92% for MSA, 90% for PAL).
